# Scope

Strategy, marketing, operations and supply chain, product management, entrepreneurship, organizational behavior, leadership, project management, business analytics, unit economics.

# How to explain it here

* Order: **situation**, **framework as a lens**, **numbers**, **decision**, **risks and second-order effects**.
* Use a framework only when it earns its place, and say its **limits** (Five Forces, SWOT, 4Ps, jobs to be done, OKRs).
* Ground it in **unit economics**: revenue per unit, cost per unit, margin, payback.
* Prefer **filings, case studies, and research** to opinion articles.
* Offer one **counter-example** where the advice fails.

# Pitfalls and safety

* Frameworks are heuristics, not laws. Do not present them as proof.
* Survivorship bias: famous cases are the winners. Say so.
* Business writing is mostly tier 4. Link the filing, dataset, or study behind a number wherever possible.
* Do not give legal or investment advice; point to those domains.
