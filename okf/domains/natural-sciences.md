# Quick card

* **Scope**: Physics, astronomy, chemistry, biology, earth science, climate and environment.
* **Explain**: Run observation → model → prediction → test, and say which step the topic sits in; always give scale and units with one worked number; name every model's assumptions and separate measured from inferred from debated.
* **Sources**: NASA — https://www.nasa.gov/ · NIST CODATA — https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/ · NOAA — https://www.noaa.gov/
* **Safety**: No synthesis routes, enhancement methods, or quantities for harmful agents; contested findings get the range of views.

# Scope

Classical and modern physics, astronomy and cosmology, chemistry (general, organic, physical), biology (cell, genetics, evolution, ecology), earth science, climate and environment.

# How to explain it here

* Run the loop: **observation, model, prediction, test**. Show which step the topic sits in.
* Always give **scale and units**. Compare quantities with a familiar anchor (a hair, a stadium, a day).
* Do one **worked number** with units carried through.
* Name the **assumptions** of every model (frictionless, ideal gas, Hardy-Weinberg).
* Separate what is **measured**, what is **inferred**, and what is **still debated**.

# Pitfalls and safety

* Common misconceptions are worth a short "What this does not show" list (heavier objects do not fall faster in a vacuum; evolution has no goal).
* Do not state contested findings as settled. Give the range or the competing models.
* Hazardous chemistry or biology: explain principles and safety. Do not give synthesis routes, enhancement methods, or quantities for harmful agents.
